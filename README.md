A hand-checked list of Free Fire tools and resources that are actually worth your time. Every link here was opened and read before it made the list.

I play Free Fire in guild matches with my squad, and every week someone asks the same three questions: where do I check a player's stats, where do I find a tournament, and what sensitivity should I use. The lists I found when I went looking were full of mod APKs, "free diamond" scams and dead links from 2021. So I made the list I wished existed.

## What is NOT on this list

This is the part that matters most. You will not find any of these here, and I will not add them no matter how many people ask:

- Mod APKs, mod menus, injectors or panels
- Diamond generators or "free diamond" sites
- Anything that asks for your Free Fire password
- Anything that breaks Garena's terms of service

Those things get accounts banned. This list is for players who want to keep their accounts.

## Official

The stuff that comes from Garena itself. When in doubt, start here.

- [Free Fire official site](https://ff.garena.com) — Garena's own home for the game: news, events, patch notes and downloads. **Free**
- [Free Fire redeem codes](https://reward.ff.garena.com) — The only safe place to redeem codes. Any other redeem site asking for your login is a scam. **Free**
- [Free Fire Advance Server](https://ff-advance.ff.garena.com) — Garena's test server. Sign up with your Facebook or Google account and try new features before they ship. **Free**
- [Garena Free Fire Global](https://www.youtube.com/@GarenaFreeFireGlobal) — The official YouTube channel: trailers, event cinematics and patch previews. **Free**
- [Garena player support](https://ffsupport.garena.com) — Account recovery, ban appeals and bug reports, straight from Garena. **Free**

## Stats and profile trackers

Look up any player by UID or nickname. Useful for scouting an opponent before a tournament match or checking your own progress. Rule of thumb: a lookup page should never ask for your password. If it does, close it.

- [FreeFireHub player tracker](https://freefirehub.com/player-tracker) — Look up any player by nickname or UID: BR and Clash Squad stats, K/D, rank and loadout. **Free**
- [Free Fire Info](https://freefireinfo.in) — UID lookup with the deep details: account creation date, Booyah Pass, guild, equipped outfits and pet. **Free**
- [Free Fire Jornal profile tool](https://freefirejornal.com/en/free-fire-profile-discover-everything-about-your-game-account-with-this-tool/) — Paste a player ID and get a full profile page: ranks, guild, likes and match history. No login needed, and never enter your password on a lookup page. **Free**
- [FF Stats](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.triptasoft.ffstats) — Android app for the same UID and nickname lookups when you would rather check on your phone. **Free**

## Sensitivity and settings

Sensitivity is personal. These tools give you a starting point — you still have to tune it in Training Mode, on your own phone, with your own fingers. Anyone selling "pro player sensitivity" as a copy-paste secret is selling you a shortcut that does not exist.

- [SensiAI Pro](https://sensiaipro.online/) — Sensitivity and DPI calculator that takes your phone's RAM, refresh rate and playstyle and gives you starting values plus HUD button sizes. Starting points only, tune them in Training Mode. **Free**
- [Free Fire sensitivity settings guide](https://www.moneycontrol.com/technology/free-fire-sensitivity-settings-important-control-settings-to-change-to-improve-your-gaming-skills-article-12905055.html) — Plain-English explainer of every sensitivity slider, with recommended ranges for beginners, rushers and snipers. **Free**
- [BlueStacks Free Fire MAX 240 FPS guide](https://www.bluestacks.com/blog/bluestacks-exclusives/ffm-high-fps-guide-en.html) — How to run Free Fire MAX at up to 240 FPS on PC through BlueStacks, with mouse sensitivity values that actually work. **Free**

## Tournaments and guilds

Where to find matches that are not just random ranked games.

- [Battlefy](https://battlefy.com) — Tournament brackets and registrations for community-run events, including Free Fire brackets. **Free**
- [Gamerji](https://www.gamerji.com) — Indian esports platform with regular Free Fire tournaments and prize pools. Some tournaments have a paid entry. **Free to join**
- [game.tv](https://game.tv) — Run or join tournaments from your phone, with Discord integration for guild events. **Free**
- [Discord](https://discord.com) — Where almost every Free Fire guild actually lives: voice comms for matches, announcements and scrims. **Free**

## Content creation

For the clips, streams and thumbnails around your matches.

- [OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com) — Free and open source recording and streaming. What most Free Fire streamers use to go live. **Free**
- [CapCut](https://www.capcut.com) — Quickest way to cut a match into a Short: auto-captions, templates, and it runs on phone and desktop. **Free with paid tier**
- [Canva](https://www.canva.com) — Thumbnails, tournament posters and guild banners without opening Photoshop. **Free with paid tier**
- [Streamlabs](https://streamlabs.com) — Overlays, alerts and widgets for your stream, plus a mobile app to stream straight from your phone. **Free with paid tier**

## Aim and practice

Aim trainers help your flicks and tracking on PC. They do not replace in-game practice on your phone, where your muscle memory actually lives.

- [Aim Lab](https://aimlab.gg) — Aim trainer with tasks you can bend towards flicks and tracking. Useful on PC days; your phone aim still needs in-game practice. **Free**
- [3D Aim Trainer](https://3daimtrainer.com) — Browser-based aim trainer, nothing to install. Good for warming up your flicks before a match. **Free**

## News, wikis and community

- [Free Fire Wiki](https://freefire.fandom.com/wiki/Free_Fire_Wiki) — Community wiki for characters, pets, weapons, maps and events. First stop when a patch drops and you want to know what changed. **Free**
- [Liquipedia Free Fire](https://liquipedia.net/freefire/Main_Page) — The competitive record: tournament results, team rosters and player pages for the pro scene. **Free**
- [r/freefire](https://www.reddit.com/r/freefire/) — The main English-speaking Free Fire community on Reddit: clips, questions, patch talk and the occasional leak that turns out real. **Free**
- [Sportskeeda Free Fire](https://www.sportskeeda.com/free-fire) — News, guides and redeem-code roundups. Solid for keeping up with events if you don't follow every creator. **Free**
- [Free Fire Mania](https://freefiremania.com.br) — Brazilian Free Fire news site, in Portuguese. Brazil is the heart of the competitive scene, so this is often where news breaks first. **Free**
- [Free Fire MAX](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.dts.freefiremax) — The game itself on Google Play, for the friend who still hasn't installed it. **Free**

## How entries are checked

Every entry in this list lives in [`links.json`](links.json) as well as in this README, so the list is data and not just prose. Two things keep it honest:

1. **Link checker.** `scripts/check_links.py` opens every URL in `links.json` and reports whether it is alive. Some big sites block automated checks (they answer 403 or 429 to scripts); those are reported as guarded, not dead, and were confirmed by hand instead. Run it yourself:

   ```bash
   python scripts/check_links.py
   ```

   The latest check report is in [`docs/link-check-2026-11-26.md`](docs/link-check-2026-11-26.md).

2. **Tests.** `tests/test_links.py` checks that every entry has a name, URL, category, cost and a real one-line note, that URLs and names are unique, that the README and `links.json` never drift apart, and that no banned word (mod, injector, diamond generator, and friends) appears anywhere in the list. Run them with:

   ```bash
   python -m pytest tests/ -q
   ```

## Contributing

Found a good resource that belongs here, or a dead link? Open an issue or a pull request. The rules are short:

- Legit only. If it can get someone's account banned, it does not go in.
- You must have opened the link yourself. No adding things from a search snippet.
- One line in the README and one entry in `links.json`, with a cost label (`Free`, `Free to join`, or `Free with paid tier`).
- Run `python -m pytest tests/ -q` and `python scripts/check_links.py` before you send it.
